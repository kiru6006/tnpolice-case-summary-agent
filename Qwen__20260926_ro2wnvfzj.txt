# 01 — Project Vision

## Problem Statement

Tamil Nadu Police submits thousands of investigation reports (FIR, Scene Mahazar, 180/183 BNSS statements, Seizure Mahazar, Medical reports, Charge Sheets under Sec 193 BNSS) to district courts and the Madras High Court annually. These submissions suffer from:

1. **Procedural gaps** — Missing mandatory documents (e.g., Magistrate confession for serious offenses)
2. **Contradictions** — Timeline mismatches between FIR, Scene Mahazar, and witness statements
3. **Bilingual complexity** — Handwritten Tamil + English + code-mixed Tanglish documents
4. **Manual triage burden** — Court clerks spend 30–45 min per case indexing and validating
5. **Delayed defect memos** — Judges issue defect notices weeks after submission, causing investigation delays
6. **Legacy law handling** — Parallel processing of BNSS (new) and CrPC/IPC/IEA (old) cases

## Vision

> Deliver a single, AI-powered platform that transforms police report evaluation from a manual, error-prone, weeks-long process into an automated, auditable, minutes-long workflow — while preserving judicial oversight and human-in-the-loop accountability.

## Mission

Build an enterprise-grade, DPDP-compliant, government-cloud-hosted AI agent system that:
- **Reads** any Tamil Nadu police investigation document (handwritten or typed)
- **Evaluates** it against BNSS/BSA/BNS and the TN Police Manual
- **Flags** gaps, contradictions, and procedural violations with source citations
- **Generates** draft Defect Memos for judicial review
- **Learns** continuously from HITL feedback

## Success Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Baseline | Target (12 mo) |
|---|---|---|
| Charge sheet completeness at submission | ~68% | ≥ 92% |
| Defect memo turnaround time | 14 days | ≤ 2 days |
| Court clerk triage time per case | 40 min | ≤ 8 min |
| AI gap-detection precision | N/A | ≥ 90% |
| AI gap-detection recall | N/A | ≥ 85% |
| Hallucination rate (legal citations) | N/A | ≤ 2% |
| Judge adoption rate (pilot district) | 0% | ≥ 75% |
| PII leakage incidents | 0 | 0 |

## Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

- ❌ Replacing judicial decision-making (guilt/innocence)
- ❌ Real-time courtroom transcription
- ❌ Predicting case outcomes or conviction rates
- ❌ Handling non-Tamil Nadu jurisdictions (Phase 1)
- ❌ Citizen-facing portal (Phase 1)
- ❌ Voice/video evidence analysis (Phase 1)

## Guiding Principles

1. **AI as assistant, not judge** — Every flag is a *suggestion* with source citations
2. **Explainability over accuracy** — A 85% accurate but explainable system beats a 95% black box
3. **Privacy by design** — PII masked before LLM inference; India-only data residency
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Simple health score first, deep drill-down on demand
5. **Continuous learning** — Monthly retraining from HITL feedback loops
