## 2. Landmark Judicial Precedents (Procedural & Evidentiary Scrutiny)

| Case Citation | Court | Relevant Legal Principle Enforced in Vetri |
|---|---|---|
| **Arnesh Kumar v. State of Bihar (2014) 8 SCC 273** | Supreme Court | Strict compliance with arrest notice and recording reasons for arrest under Sec 41/41A CrPC (now Sec 35 BNSS). |
| **Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v. Kailash Kushanrao Gorantyal (2020) 7 SCC 1** | Supreme Court | Mandatory production of electronic record certificate (Sec 65B IEA, now Sec 63 BSA) as condition precedent for digital evidence. |
| **Lalita Kumari v. Govt. of U.P. (2014) 2 SCC 1** | Supreme Court | Mandatory registration of FIR under Sec 154 CrPC (now Sec 173 BNSS) in all cognizable offenses. |
| **State of Haryana v. Bhajan Lal 1992 Supp (1) SCC 335** | Supreme Court | Grounds for quashing charge sheets and standards for prima facie procedural validity. |
| **K. Ramajayam v. State (Madras HC 2016)** | Madras High Court | Strict duty of court clerks and judicial magistrates to reject incomplete police dossiers under Rule 25 of Criminal Rules of Practice. |
